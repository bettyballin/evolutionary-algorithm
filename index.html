Fitness Landscape

The fitness landscape represents the mapping from genotype (solution) to fitness value. Peaks represent high fitness, while valleys represent low fitness. The algorithm aims to find the highest peak.

Population Diversity

This visualization shows the diversity of the population over generations. High diversity means solutions are spread out in the search space, while low diversity indicates convergence.

Genetic Operations

Mutation introduces small random changes to individuals, enabling exploration of the search space. Crossover combines genetic material from two parents to create offspring, enabling exploitation of good solutions.

Selection Pressure

Selection pressure determines how strongly fitness influences selection for reproduction. Higher pressure favors fitter individuals, potentially leading to premature convergence. Lower pressure maintains diversity but may slow convergence.

Strategy Comparison

This visualization compares different evolutionary strategies (combinations of selection methods, mutation rates, etc.) on the same problem, showing how they affect convergence speed and solution quality.
